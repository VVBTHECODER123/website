Biography

Prof. Rakesh Batabyal is a distinguished historian specializing in modern and contemporary history, intellectual movements, media studies, and institutional development in South Asia and Central/Eastern Europe.

Published Works

Author 2024

Idea of Order: Perspectives from Central and Eastern Europe (1989–2022)

Examines political and economic transformations in Central and Eastern Europe following the end of the socialist era.

Author 2005

Communalism in Bengal: From Famine to Partition (1932–1947)

Critical study detailing the social, political, and institutional trajectory leading to the 1947 partition of Bengal.

Author 2014

JNU: The Making of a University

A definitive history exploring the institutional design, intellectual ethos, and cultural impact of JNU.

Editor 2007

The Penguin Book of Modern Indian Speeches

Curated anthology tracing oratorical milestones and political thought across modern Indian history.

Author

The Modern School: A History

Historical analysis examining educational developments, institutional heritage, and modern pedagogy.
